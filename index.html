About Me

I am an Operations and Supply Chain Management student at the University of Utah with an interest in operations management, process improvement, and business strategy. My professional experience in retail leadership and industrial supply has helped me understand how effective systems, data, and standardized processes can improve employee performance and customer outcomes. I am especially interested in how artificial intelligence can support business analysis, automate routine tasks, improve decision-making, and identify opportunities for greater efficiency. I also want to continue developing my technical abilities in web development, data analytics, information systems, and Microsoft Excel. My long-term goal is to combine business knowledge with technology to improve organizational processes and help teams operate more effectively.

Projects

GE Business Consulting Project

Collaborated with a student consulting team to analyze General Electric's operations across major business sectors, including healthcare, aerospace, and energy. We researched GE's position within each sector, identified opportunities for improvement, and developed strategic recommendations intended to strengthen the company's overall business performance.

MGT 3030

Fastenal Sourcing Process Capstone

Identified an opportunity to improve Fastenal's sourcing process by giving branch employees clearer access to sourcing knowledge, tools, and procedures. I created a standardized digital resource with practical guidance and video tutorials designed to improve request quality, employee efficiency, customer outcomes, and speed to revenue.

Fastenal Internship

Utah Watershed Research Project

Collected water samples from several sources throughout Utah and measured their levels of E. coli bacteria. The project examined differences in water quality and explored how population growth and human development can affect watersheds and access to clean water resources.

BIOL 2015

Fairlife Milk Ethics Project

Researched ethical concerns involving Fairlife milk and examined the broader effects of factory farming within the dairy industry. The project considered animal welfare, corporate responsibility, consumer trust, and how large-scale agricultural practices can affect the ethical reputation of a company and its products.
